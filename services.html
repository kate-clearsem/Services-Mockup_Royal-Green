Turf Maintenance

South Florida lawns face heat, drought, heavy rain, insects, and persistent weed pressure. Our proactive residential program is designed to build healthier turf over time, rather than relying on a one-time treatment. Our Basic Lawn Program is typically delivered at 6–7 week intervals and consists of seven rounds of treatment over the course of a year.

  • Fertilizer, insect control, and broadleaf and grassy weed management throughout the program
  • Full-lawn treatments for turf insects and broadleaf weeds, rather than spot treating only visible areas
  • Fertilizer blends selected for your lawn and adjusted on site when conditions call for it
  • Service calls between scheduled visits when needed

Tree & Shrub Maintenance

Florida’s sandy, often nutrient-poor soils and year-round pest and disease pressures can challenge tropical and subtropical plants. An ISA Certified Arborist oversees plant-health recommendations and custom fertilizer blends.

  • Quarterly, proactive maintenance
  • Custom fertilizer and chelated micronutrients for ornamental plants
  • Preventive and responsive insect, mite, and fungal disease management
  • Technicians with State, DACS Fertilizer, and Green Industries BMP certifications

Interior & Exterior Pest Control

The initial Royal Flush service includes a thorough inspection inside and out to identify pest activity, nesting or harborage areas, and possible entry points.

  • Minor exclusion work, such as sealing small gaps, where appropriate
  • Baits and dusts placed in areas not accessible to children and pets, including attic bait where appropriate
  • Exterior perimeter treatment to help deter pests from entering
  • Removal of accessible spiderwebs and wasp or mud-dauber nests

Flea and tick treatment is provided for active infestations and may cost extra. Rodent control is an additional service. Explore common household pests.
