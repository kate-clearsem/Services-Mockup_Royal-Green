Turf Health

Lawn Care & Fertilization

Why is my Florida lawn turning brown or yellow?
Discoloration can result from fungal disease, insect damage, nutrient deficiencies, irrigation, or environmental stress. Because symptoms can look similar, identifying the underlying cause is important before selecting a treatment.
How often should my lawn be fertilized in South Florida?
Schedules depend on grass type, soil conditions, seasonal growth, and local regulations. Royal Green's standard lawn care program includes seven treatments annually, generally spaced six to seven weeks apart.
What is included in Royal Green’s lawn treatment program?
The program focuses on turf health through fertilization, weed management, and monitoring for insects and disease. Treatments are selected based on lawn condition, the season, and the customer's service plan.
Can too much rain or irrigation damage my lawn?
Yes. Excessive moisture can saturate soil, limit oxygen available to roots, and encourage certain fungal diseases. Proper irrigation management is especially important in South Florida.
Diagnosis & Treatment

Lawn Diseases & Landscape Pests

What causes brown patches in St. Augustine grass?
Brown patches can be caused by fungal diseases, chinch bugs, irrigation problems, nutrient deficiencies, or environmental stress. A professional inspection can help identify the cause.
How can I tell if my lawn has fungus or insect damage?
Fungal diseases may cause discolored patches, spots on grass blades, or thinning turf. Insect damage can appear as irregular yellow or brown areas. Since symptoms overlap, an inspection can help determine what is happening.
What are common lawn pests in South Florida?
Common turf pests include chinch bugs, sod webworms, armyworms, and various grubs. They may cause thinning, discoloration, or bare areas.
How long does it take to see results from professional lawn treatments?
Results depend on the condition of the lawn and the problem being addressed. Some treatments may show effects relatively quickly, while improvements in turf density and color can take longer.
Ornamental Care

Tree, Shrub & Ornamental Care

Why are the leaves on my trees or shrubs turning yellow?
Yellowing may indicate nutrient deficiencies, improper watering, insect damage, disease, or environmental stress. South Florida soil conditions can also affect nutrient availability.
Do palm trees need special fertilization in Florida?
Florida palms often require balanced nutrition with nutrients such as potassium, magnesium, and manganese. Proper nutrition supports healthy growth and helps prevent common deficiencies.
How often should trees and shrubs be treated?
Treatment schedules depend on plant species, growing conditions, and pest or disease activity. Royal Green offers a quarterly tree and shrub care program.
Can Royal Green help identify plant diseases and nutrient deficiencies?
Our team has experience assessing plant health concerns, including insect infestations, fungal diseases, and nutrient issues. Contact us to discuss your landscape.
